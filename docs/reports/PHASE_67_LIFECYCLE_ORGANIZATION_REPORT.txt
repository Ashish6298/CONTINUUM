1. OBJECTIVES AND SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Phase 67 completes domain lifecycle transitions, tag organization, custom types,
optional collections, recent/chronological sorting, related-activity links, and
archived activity recovery.

Key requirements addressed:
1. Complete Status Lifecycle: ACTIVE, PAUSED, COMPLETED, ARCHIVED with explicit
   valid transitions and strict rejection of illegal state jumps.
2. Reopen & Recovery: COMPLETED activities can reopen to ACTIVE; ARCHIVED
   activities remain fully preserved and recoverable to ACTIVE or PAUSED.
3. Separation of Status and History: Lifecycle status changes update only activity
   metadata and state indices; historical checkpoints are never deleted or rewritten.
4. Custom Types & Dynamic Tags: Arbitrary activity types supported (built-in coding,
   study, research, writing, work_task, ai_agent, plus custom string types).
   Tags are normalized, lowercase, and deduplicated.
5. Collections: Optional grouping of activities under named collections, with
   aggregated counts and filtering.
6. Related Activity Links: Bidirectional and unidirectional relations between
   activities without contaminating historical state.
7. Recent Sorting & Bounded Pagination: Sorting by 'recent' (updated_at desc),
   'created', or 'title' with strict limit/offset bounds (I-17).
8. REST Endpoints: Added handlers and routes for:
   - GET /api/v1/activities (enhanced filtering, collections, tags, sorting)
   - GET /api/v1/collections (aggregated collection statistics)
   - GET /api/v1/tags (aggregated tag statistics)
   - POST /api/v1/activities/transition (status lifecycle transitions)
   - POST /api/v1/activities/update (metadata/tag/collection/type edits)
   - POST /api/v1/activities/links (linking and unlinking activities)
